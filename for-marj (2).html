Dear Marj,

I'm not going to pretend nothing happened. I said I was tired of you. I said I wasn't ready. And I know exactly how that must have landed — like confirmation of every quiet doubt you've ever had about us.

But here's what was actually true underneath all of it: I was tired of falling short. Of watching the distance swallow things I didn't know how to say. Of showing up to our conversations already depleted and then taking it out in the worst way possible. I said the cruelest version of what I was feeling and aimed it straight at you. That was mine. That's on me.

You've never been too much, Marj. You've been patient with someone who didn't always deserve it.

I'm not here to give you a speech or make a list of promises. I'm just asking for a chance to mean something different going forward. Not because I have everything figured out, but because I know exactly how I feel when I imagine you not being in my life anymore — and it's not relief.

Lets give me another shot, Marj. I meant everything that I have said in the past and even in the future. We can get through heverything Marj.

always yours,
